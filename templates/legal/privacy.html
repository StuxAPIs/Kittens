Privacy Policy

Kittens serves images. It doesn't ask you to sign up, log in, or hand over any personal information.

What we collect

Standard web server logs (IP address, requested path, user agent, timestamp) for operating and securing the service. These logs are not sold, shared, or used for tracking, and are rotated out periodically.

What we don't collect

No accounts, no analytics trackers, no advertising identifiers, no cookies (see the Cookies Policy).

Third parties

The images served by this API are hosted on our own infrastructure. No third-party services see your requests except whatever network sits between you and our server (your ISP, any CDN in front of us).
